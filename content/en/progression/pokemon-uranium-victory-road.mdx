Pokemon Uranium Victory Road is the last stretch of Tandor you'll cross before the Championship Site, and it is easily the most punishing area in the game. Unlike the short routes you breezed through earlier, this dungeon stacks three distinct zones — an outdoor ridge, a sprawling cave, and a volcanic interior — into one unforgiving gauntlet.

If you're stuck here, you're not alone. Community reports consistently name Pokemon Uranium Victory Road as the place where players either soft-lock themselves or lose a run to a trainer battle they never saw coming. This guide breaks down the layout, the wild encounters, the items, and the trainers so you can walk into the Championship Site with a full team and a clear head.

## Where Is Victory Road in Pokemon Uranium?

Victory Road is the game's final dungeon. It sits southwest of Legen Town and southeast of the Championship Site, which means you'll pass through it on the way to your final battles. The entrance nearest to Legen Town is the one most players use first, and the bridge outside links the two cave mouths together.

One detail that trips people up: the easternmost cave entrance leads out to Route 9, not deeper into the dungeon. If you take that exit expecting progress, you'll end up back on a normal route wondering what went wrong. Treat the outdoor area as a hub, and the cave plus volcano as the real content.

The dungeon is also home to a Legendary encounter. Actan can be found by climbing the waterfall beside the entrance to the cave portion of Victory Road — a spot that's easy to walk past if you're focused on the Strength boulder in front of you.

## Victory Road Layout: Three Zones, One Dungeon

The area is divided into an outdoor section, a cave section, and a volcano section known as Mt. Actanite. Each zone has its own wild encounter table, its own level range, and its own set of hazards.

| Zone | Key Feature | Wild Level Range | Suggested Team Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outdoor ridge & bridge | Links both cave entrances; ninja trainers on the bridge | 36–42 | 45+ |
| Cave section | Strength boulder gate; Terlard and Drilgann | 48–52 | 48–52 |
| Volcano section (Mt. Actanite) | Volcanic interior; Firoke and Devimp | 25–59 | 55+ |
| Secret Garden | Optional double battle against Sol & Chelsea | N/A (trainer only) | 70+ |

Notice the level jump. The outdoor area is tuned for teams in the low 40s, but the cave throws level 48–52 wild Pokemon at you immediately, and the volcano pushes toward the high 50s. If you arrive underleveled, you'll spend more turns healing than exploring — which matters a lot given the soft-lock warning below.

## Before You Enter: Strength, the Power Glove, and Soft-Lock Warnings

This is the single most important section of any Pokemon Uranium Victory Road guide. According to the community wiki, advancing too far into the cave section without a Pokemon that knows Strength — or without the Power Glove — will leave you unable to progress or leave unless your entire party faints. The only other escape is using Teleport or Fly from the bridge outside both cave entrances.

In other words: check your party before you step inside. A Strength user or the Power Glove is not optional, it's a hard requirement.

There are also a few known bugs that player experience has documented extensively:

| Hazard | What Happens | How to Avoid It |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Using Escape Rope, Dig, or Quantum Leap in the first cave | The sky turns black | Exit on foot, or Fly/Teleport from the bridge |
| Battling the two Secret Garden trainers with only one Pokemon alive | The game crashes | Heal to a full party before the fight |
| Skipping Strength / Power Glove | Soft-lock in the cave | Verify your party before entering |

None of these are run-ending if you plan for them, but all three are frustrating to discover the hard way. Save before entering the cave section, and keep a backup save if you're playing a long session.

## Items in Victory Road

Victory Road isn't overflowing with loot, but the two items here are worth a detour.

| Item | Location |
| --- | --- |
| TM37 Sandstorm | Behind a rock east of the Legen Town gate |
| Full Restore | At the end of a narrow passage south of the cave leading to Route 9 |

The Full Restore is the more valuable of the two for most players, since it's a free full heal during the hardest stretch of the game. Grab it on your way toward the Route 9 exit rather than doubling back for it later.

## Trainers in Pokemon Uranium Victory Road

Trainer battles here are where unprepared teams fall apart. The most notable fights are Ace Trainer Elsa and the optional double battle against Sol & Chelsea in the Secret Garden.

**Ace Trainer Elsa**

| Pokemon | Level | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpico | 60 | $5,700 |
| Anderind | 60 | — |

Both Pokemon carry no held items, which keeps the fight straightforward — but level 60 is a real step up from the cave's wild encounters. Community trivia notes that Elsa's name is likely a nod to the Disney film *Frozen*.

**Elite Trainers Sol & Chelsea (Secret Garden)**

This is a double battle, and it's optional — but it's also the toughest fight in the dungeon. Sol runs Grass types, Chelsea runs Water types, and the reward is $8,030 plus a Sprinklotad.

Two Mega Stones are in play here — Whimsicottite and Syrentideite — so expect Mega Evolutions mid-fight. Sunny Day and Rain Dance also stack against you, boosting Sol's Grass moves and Chelsea's Water moves respectively. Bring a Fire or Flying answer for the Grass side and an Electric or Grass answer for the Water side.

The wiki also notes a few interesting quirks worth knowing. Chelsea's Daikatuna is the only Pokemon in the game that knows Razor Shell, and Sketch is the only way to use that move elsewhere outside of Metronome. The Nami Ninja's Luchabra runs a different moveset than expected, and Beauty Amber carries a level 64 Laissure that shouldn't exist without hacking since Terrabbit evolves at level 66.

One more thing: the two ninjas on the bridge can be fought as a double battle by walking between them, or one at a time by talking to each individually. Player experience suggests fighting them separately yields more money overall.

## Tips for Clearing Victory Road

- **Pack a Strength user.** This is non-negotiable unless you have the Power Glove.
- **Heal before the Secret Garden.** A crash bug triggers if you challenge those trainers with only one Pokemon alive.
- **Skip Escape Rope and Dig in the first cave.** The black-sky bug is purely visual, but it's alarming and avoidable.
- **Climb the waterfall early.** Actan is easy to miss if you rush toward the cave mouth.
- **Level to 55+ before the volcano.** Firoke at 50–59 will outpace an underleveled team.
- **Save often.** This is the last dungeon; there's no reason to lose progress here.

## FAQ

**Do I need Strength to finish Pokemon Uranium Victory Road?**
Yes. Without a Pokemon that knows Strength — or the Power Glove — you can get stuck deep in the cave section with no way out except fainting your whole team or using Fly or Teleport from the bridge outside. Check your party before entering.

**Where do I find Actan in Victory Road?**
Actan is located by climbing the waterfall beside the entrance to the cave portion of Victory Road. It's easy to overlook, so sweep the outdoor area before heading into the cave.

**Is the Secret Garden double battle optional?**
It is. Sol & Chelsea only fight you if you enter the Secret Garden. That said, the reward of $8,030 plus a Sprinklotad makes it worth doing once your team is around level 70.

**What level should my team be for Victory Road?**
Around level 50 for the cave section and 55–60 for the volcano. For the Sol & Chelsea double battle, aim for the low 70s — their team ranges from level 72 to 73 and includes two Mega Evolutions.
