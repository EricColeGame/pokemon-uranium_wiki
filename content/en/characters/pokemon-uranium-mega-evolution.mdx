Pokemon Uranium mega evolution is one of the most rewarding systems in the entire Tandor region, and it is also one of the easiest to overlook. It turns a familiar partner into a battle-ending threat for a few turns — a stat jump, a new ability, and a fresh look — but only if you have the right stone in the right hands. This guide walks through how Pokemon Uranium mega evolution actually works, which Pokémon can use it, and where the documented Mega Stones are hiding.

## What Is Mega Evolution in Pokemon Uranium?

In Tandor, a Mega Evolution is a temporary transformation that happens inside a battle rather than on a level-up screen. The Pokémon changes appearance, gains a boost to its stats, and sometimes picks up a completely different ability — but the change only holds for the duration of the fight, or until that Pokémon faints. The moment the battle ends, everything snaps back to normal.

The rule that shapes every decision is simple: only one Pokémon can Mega Evolve per battle. If your Mega is knocked out, it reverts to its original form and cannot be Mega Evolved again in that same battle. That makes your choice of Mega a commitment rather than a rotation, and it is the single biggest reason team building around it takes planning.

| Rule | What It Means in Practice |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | Active until the battle ends or the Pokémon faints |
| Battle limit | Only one Mega Evolution per battle |
| Fainting | Reverts immediately and cannot Mega Evolve again in that battle |
| Requirements | Trainer must hold a Key Stone; the Pokémon must hold its own Mega Stone |
| Stat effect | Immediate stat increase for as long as the form lasts |
| Ability effect | Some Mega forms gain a brand-new ability |

## How to Unlock Mega Evolution: Key Stones and the Mega Bracelet

Pokemon Uranium mega evolution cannot be triggered at all until your trainer is carrying a Key Stone. In Uranium, that Key Stone is the Mega Bracelet, awarded by Vaeryn after you defeat him at the Snowbank Town Gym. He also hands over one free Mega Stone that matches a Pokémon already in your party, which is a genuine head start in the middle of a playthrough.

That free stone is a shortcut, not the only route. Community reports note that before patch 1.0.2, the gift was the only way to obtain the Unidentified Fallen Object used to Mega Evolve S51-A. In current releases, every usable Mega Stone can be found somewhere in Tandor, so the freebie is convenience rather than necessity.

There is one clear exception. Vaeryn will not provide stones for S51-A's Mothership Form, Dark Dramsama, or Garlikid — those have to be tracked down the hard way.

| Step | What to Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Defeat Vaeryn at the Snowbank Town Gym | Earns the Mega Bracelet, your Key Stone |
| 2 | Claim the free Mega Stone | It matches a Pokémon already in your party |
| 3 | Give the stone to the matching species | Only that species can use that particular stone |
| 4 | Enter a battle and choose the Mega Evolution command | Triggers the transformation mid-fight |
| 5 | Hunt down the remaining stones across Tandor | Lets you build a team around more than one option |

If you want to verify stone locations as patches roll out, the [official Pokemon Uranium website](https://pokemonuranium.org/) and the community's [Mega Evolution page on the Pokemon Uranium Wiki](https://pokemon-uranium.fandom.com/wiki/Mega_Evolution) are the two most reliable references.

## Every Pokemon Uranium Mega Evolution and Its Mega Stone

Two species anchor the system for most players, and both sit at the end of Tandor's starter lines. Their typing does not change when they transform — only their stats and abilities do.

| Pokémon | Type | Base Ability | Mega Ability | Mega Stone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metalynx | Grass / Steel | Battle Armor | Heatproof | Metalynxite |

A handful of additional forms are documented by the community but fall outside Vaeryn's gift:

- **S51-A (Mothership Form)** — tied to the Unidentified Fallen Object rather than a standard stone hand-out.
- **Dark Dramsama** — an alternate form with its own stone that Vaeryn will not supply.
- **Garlikid** — a Mega form whose stone also has to be found independently.

Because those three sit outside the standard gift, expect to spend real time exploring Tandor if you want them on your roster.

## Mega Stone Locations in Tandor

The two most commonly used stones are sold rather than hidden, which makes them the easiest entry point into the system.

| Mega Stone | Where to Find It | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metalynxite | Bealbeach Department Store, 5F | Wednesdays |
| Archillesite | Bealbeach Department Store, 5F | Mondays |

Department store stock rotates by day of the week, so a Wednesday errand and a Monday errand are two separate trips — plan them around your gym route. The remaining usable stones are scattered across the region as field finds and event rewards. Player experience suggests collecting them well before the final stretch of gyms, since a stone sitting in your bag does nothing for you.

## What Actually Changes: Stats, Abilities, Height, and Weight

Uranium keeps things simple in one respect: height never changes during Mega Evolution, which surprises players used to the official games. Weight does change, and the difference is easy to spot on the two flagship forms.

| No. | Pokémon | Normal Weight | Mega Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 002 | Metalynx | 125 kg (275.5 lb) | 146.5 kg (323 lb) |
| 004 | Archilles | 85.5 kg (188.5 lb) | 88.7 kg (195.6 lb) |

The ability swap is where the real value sits, because each Mega trades a defensive or contact-based ability for something that changes how the whole battle plays out.

| Pokémon | Base Ability | Mega Ability | Practical Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metalynx | Battle Armor | Heatproof | Critical hits are blocked before; Fire damage is halved after |
| Archilles | Flame Body | Drought | Contact moves may burn before; harsh sunlight is set after |

Heatproof patches the single biggest hole in a Grass/Steel profile, which is a weakness to Fire. Drought, meanwhile, powers up Fire-type moves and weakens Water-type moves for everyone on the field, so it rewards a team built to exploit sun rather than a lone attacker.

## Battle Tips for Building Around Mega Evolution

Pokemon Uranium mega evolution only allows one transformation per battle, so treat it as a resource you spend, not a button you press whenever a fight gets scary.

- **Choose your Mega before the battle starts.** Decide which Pokémon will carry the stone and what job it needs to do, then build the rest of the team to support that plan.
- **Budget the held item slot.** A Mega Stone occupies the item slot, so you give up Leftovers, Choice items, and berries on that Pokémon.
- **Build around the new ability, not the old one.** Mega Archilles wants sun-abusing teammates; Mega Metalynx wants a partner that covers Ground and Fighting.
- **Hunt stones early.** Metalynxite and Archillesite are cheap and available at Bealbeach, so there is no reason to enter the late game without at least one.
- **Have a fallback win condition.** If your Mega faints, it is gone for that battle — keep a second sweeper or a defensive core ready.
- **Use the free stone wisely.** Whatever Vaeryn gives you matches your current party, so load up on the Pokémon you actually intend to Mega Evolve.

| If Your Team Needs... | Consider | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A bulky answer to Fire-types | Mega Metalynx | Heatproof halves Fire damage on a Grass/Steel body |
| Sun support for Fire moves | Mega Archilles | Drought sets harsh sunlight the moment it transforms |
| A safe mid-game Mega | Either starter Mega | Both stones are available early at Bealbeach |

A good rule of thumb is to test your Mega pick in a few trainer battles before committing to a gym run. If the transformation does not flip a matchup you were losing, the stone is probably better spent elsewhere.

## FAQ

**How many Pokémon can Mega Evolve in a single battle in Pokemon Uranium?**
Only one. Once a Pokémon transforms, no other member of your party can Mega Evolve during that battle, and a fainted Mega cannot transform again for the rest of the fight.

**How do you get the Mega Bracelet in Pokemon Uranium?**
Defeat Vaeryn at the Snowbank Town Gym. He awards the Mega Bracelet as your Key Stone and also gives you one free Mega Stone that matches a Pokémon currently in your party.

**Can you Mega Evolve S51-A, Dark Dramsama, or Garlikid?**
Yes, but Vaeryn will not supply their stones. S51-A's Mothership Form, Dark Dramsama, and Garlikid all require you to find their stones elsewhere in Tandor.

**Does Pokemon Uranium mega evolution change a Pokémon's height or weight?**
Height stays the same. Weight increases: Metalynx rises from 125 kg (275.5 lb) to 146.5 kg (323 lb), while Archilles goes from 85.5 kg (188.5 lb) to 88.7 kg (195.6 lb).
