## What an Ability Capsule Does in Pokémon Uranium

An Ability Capsule is a single-use item that flips a Pokémon between its two **standard** abilities. If the individual you caught rolled the wrong one of its two normal abilities for your build, the capsule is the fastest fix in the game: no breeding, no egg moves, and no leveling a replacement from scratch.

What it does *not* do is reach hidden abilities. That's the single most common misunderstanding in the community, and it mirrors the rule in the mainline games, where a separate item was later introduced specifically for the hidden pool. Uranium's wiki keeps hidden abilities on their own page for exactly that reason.

| Action | Supported? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Switch between a Pokémon's two standard abilities | Yes | This is the item's entire purpose |
| Switch to or from a hidden ability | No | Hidden abilities use a separate system |
| Change a Pokémon that has only one ability | No | There's no second slot to swap into |
| Make the change permanent | Yes | The swap sticks to that individual |
| Be reused | No | One capsule, one swap |
| Be reversed with a second capsule | Yes (community reports) | A second capsule typically flips it back |

### Standard abilities vs. hidden abilities

Every species in Uranium has a set of possible abilities. Most have one or two standard abilities that show up on ordinary wild catches. Hidden abilities sit outside that pool, which is precisely why they're desirable — and precisely why players assume a capsule must be the shortcut.

| Ability type | Appears on wild catches? | Capsule can reach it? | Typical route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard ability #1 | Yes | Yes | Catch, then swap if needed |
| Standard ability #2 | Yes | Yes | Catch, then swap if needed |
| Hidden ability | Rarely, or via special sources | No | Breeding, trading, or in-game events |

### Why the confusion exists

Players coming from later generations of the official games often assume one item handles both jobs, because that's how it works there. Community reports about Uranium are consistent: the two mechanics are separate, and the wiki documents them separately. So before you plan a build around a rare ability, check which category it falls into.

**Bottom line:** the Pokemon Uranium ability capsule solves the "right species, wrong normal ability" problem. It does not solve the "I want the rare ability" problem, and no amount of capsule farming will change that.

## How to Use a Pokemon Uranium Ability Capsule (Step by Step)

Order matters more than the item itself here. Follow this sequence and you'll dodge the most common regret in the game.

| Step | Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Save your game before touching the item | There's no built-in undo if you misjudge |
| 2 | Open the summary screen and note the current ability | You need a baseline to confirm the change |
| 3 | Confirm the species has two standard abilities | A one-ability Pokémon wastes the capsule |
| 4 | Use the capsule from your bag on that Pokémon | The swap applies immediately |
| 5 | Re-open the summary and verify the new ability | Confirm you landed on the other slot |
| 6 | If it's the wrong one, apply a second capsule | Community reports say it flips back |

Treat the Pokemon Uranium ability capsule as a build tool, not a lottery ticket. Decide what you need *before* you use it. A defensive wall usually wants passive damage reduction or status immunity, while a fast attacker wants an offensive or Speed-related ability. If neither of your Pokémon's two standard abilities fits the role you're building, a capsule won't rescue the build — breed a different individual instead.

One practical note: many players burn a capsule, then realize the real problem was a mismatched nature or a sloppy EV spread. Fix the fundamentals first. An ability swap amplifies a good build; it rarely rescues a bad one.

## Which Team Roles Benefit Most From a Swap

You don't need a species-by-species list to decide whether a swap is worth it. You need to know what your team is missing.

| Team role | Ability trait you want | Why the capsule is worth it |
|---|---|---|
| Physical wall | Status immunity, passive recovery, damage reduction | Turns a mediocre defensive pick into a reliable pivot |
| Special sweeper | Power or Speed boost, priority immunity | Raises the damage ceiling directly |
| Pivot / switch unit | Switch-out utility, hazard immunity | Improves momentum on every turn |
| Weather or field team | Weather-setting or weather-synergy ability | Enables the whole strategy, not just one slot |
| Nuclear-type user | Defensive immunity or resistance support | Nuclear-types are fragile; the right ability extends their life |
| Doubles support | Redirection, stat support, ally healing | Often the difference between good and great support |

A simple test before you commit: ask whether the new ability changes how opponents must play around that Pokémon. If the answer is no, save the capsule. If the answer is yes — they now have to carry a coverage move or lead differently — you've found a good use.

## Tips, Mistakes, and Save Safety

- **Back up your save file.** Uranium is a fan project, and unusual item interactions are the classic way to lose progress. Copy your save folder before any big swap.
- **Don't capsule a Pokémon you plan to replace.** If you're still deciding between two teammates, wait until the roster is settled.
- **Check the ability, not the nature.** Confirm which of the two problems you actually have before spending anything.
- **Breeding beats capsules for hidden abilities.** There is no shortcut around the hidden pool.
- **Budget them like rare TMs.** One swap per capsule, and they aren't unlimited.
- **Test in a safe battle first.** Confirm the new ability triggers the way you expect before you build a strategy around it.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Pokemon Uranium Ability Capsule

**Can a Pokemon Uranium ability capsule give my Pokémon a hidden ability?**

No. A Pokemon Uranium ability capsule only moves a Pokémon between its two standard abilities. Hidden abilities come from a separate system — breeding, trading, or specific in-game sources — and no capsule will produce one.

**What happens if my Pokémon only has one standard ability?**

Nothing. There's no second slot to switch into, so the capsule is wasted. Check the summary screen or the wiki before you use it.

**Is a capsule better than breeding a new Pokémon?**

It's faster and cheaper for standard abilities, because you keep the individual you've already trained — nature, EVs, level, and friendship all carry over. Breeding remains the only route to hidden abilities.

**Can I undo a capsule if I get the wrong ability?**

In the official games, a second capsule flips the ability back, and community reports say Uranium behaves the same way. Save before you use one anyway, because a backup costs nothing and a wasted item does.
