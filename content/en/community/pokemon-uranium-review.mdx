## What Is Pokemon Uranium, Exactly?

Pokémon Uranium is a free, fan-made RPG built in RPG Maker XP on top of the Pokémon Essentials framework. It is set in the Tandor region, a map that swings from tropical coastlines to industrial wastelands, and it comes with its own gym circuit, a championship run, and a Pokédex full of original species.

Development began back in 2007, and version 1.0 launched on August 6, 2016. Within days, Nintendo of America issued takedown notices against the official download links. The team complied, distribution moved underground, and active development eventually stopped. The full sequence of events is documented in the [Wikipedia entry for Pokémon Uranium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Uranium), which is worth a read if you care about fan-game history.

## Pokemon Uranium Review: Gameplay, Difficulty, and Pacing

At its core, this is classic Pokémon. You pick a starter, collect eight badges, beat a villainous organization, and challenge a champion. Battles are turn-based, double battles show up regularly, and the type chart matters more than flashy gimmicks.

What separates Uranium from a romhack is how much original content it carries. There are new moves, new abilities, a full custom soundtrack, and a Nuclear type that functions like a nuclear reactor: Nuclear attacks hit most types for punishing super-effective damage, but Nuclear Pokémon themselves are fragile and can be punished hard. It is a genuinely interesting risk-reward layer that official games have never attempted.

The pacing is where opinions split. Community reports consistently describe the level curve as steep, with long stretches of wild-Pokémon grinding between gyms. Trainer rematches are limited, and money for healing items runs thin in the mid game. One player experience described the game as "insanely grindy" while still calling it amazing overall — a contradiction that sums up Uranium better than any single score.

## New Pokemon, Types, and the Tandor Region

The headline feature of any Pokemon Uranium review has to be the roster. Uranium adds more than 150 original species, which is roughly the size of a first-generation Pokédex. The three starters — Raptorch, Orchynx, and Eletux — each evolve into fully realized three-stage lines, and the regional bird and rodent equivalents get the same treatment.

Tandor itself is a strong setting. The region moves from beach towns to nuclear facilities, and the story leans into mutation and environmental damage in a way that feels darker than the mainline games. Gyms are built around original type combinations, so you cannot simply copy a walkthrough team and coast.

## Presentation: Graphics, Music, and Performance

Visually, Uranium aims for the DS-era Pokémon look and mostly lands it. Sprite work is clean, overworld maps are detailed, and battle animations are serviceable. The custom soundtrack is the standout — it is fully original and frequently praised as one of the best parts of the package.

Performance is another story. Because it runs on RPG Maker XP, the game is locked to older Windows builds in practice, and players report crashes, soft-locks, and save-corruption risks. There is no auto-save, no modern quality-of-life layer, and no official patching anymore.

## How to Play Pokemon Uranium Safely Today

Since official distribution ended, getting the game requires more caution than it used to. Torrent sites and random file hosts are the most common source of problems, and community reports warn specifically about corrupted or bundled-malware copies.

If you are not comfortable with that risk profile, that is a completely reasonable position. Uranium is a genuinely impressive achievement, but it is also an abandoned piece of software running on an old engine.

## FAQ

**Is Pokemon Uranium free to play?**
Yes. It was always a free fan project with no monetization, microtransactions, or paid expansions. Any site charging you money for it is not a legitimate source.

**Is Pokemon Uranium still available in 2026?**
Not through official channels. The original download links were removed after a takedown notice in 2016, so anyone searching for a Pokemon Uranium review and a download today will be relying on community mirrors and archives. Treat those files carefully.

**How many new Pokémon does Pokemon Uranium add?**
More than 150 original species, including three full starter lines, a Nuclear-type Eeveelution, and a legendary tied to the main story. That is roughly the size of an entire first-generation Pokédex.

**Is Pokemon Uranium harder than the official games?**
Generally yes, mostly because of the level curve rather than clever design. Most Pokemon Uranium review write-ups mention grinding as the biggest barrier, and boss battles expect properly trained, well-balanced teams.
