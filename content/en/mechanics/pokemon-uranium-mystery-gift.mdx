Every so often, the developers behind Pokemon Uranium quietly drop free Pokémon and items into the hands of anyone who knows where to look. The **Pokemon Uranium Mystery Gift** system is the mechanism behind those anniversary and holiday giveaways, and it costs nothing to use — no codes, no online subscription, no trades required. If you have never opened it, you are leaving free team members, held items, and evolution stones sitting on the table. This guide walks through the **Pokemon Uranium Mystery Gift** feature from start to finish: how to unlock it, where to collect your rewards, and how to soft-reset for the nature or ability you actually want.

## What Is the Mystery Gift Feature in Pokemon Uranium?

Mystery Gift is a built-in distribution feature that hands out special Gift Pokémon and items, usually to commemorate a holiday, a game anniversary, or a community milestone for the fan-made project. Each distribution is packaged as a Wonder Card — a small digital voucher that records what you received and when. You claim the card from the title screen, then pick up the actual creature or item from a shop NPC inside your save file.

Because the developers deliver these gifts through the game client itself rather than a console online service, there is nothing to sign up for and no redemption code to type in. You simply update the game, open the menu, and claim.

| Typical contents | Event Pokémon, held items, evolution items, Rare Candies |
| Save requirement | You must enter the game and save after claiming |
| Online account needed | No |

## How to Claim a Pokemon Uranium Mystery Gift

The process is short, but it has one step that trips up almost everyone: the save. Follow the sequence below in order and you will not have to re-claim anything.

| Step | Action | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Progress until Professor Bamb'o gives you the Pokédex | The menu option stays hidden before this point |
| 2 | Quit to the Main Menu (before loading a save file) | The Mystery Gift option lives on the title screen |
| 3 | Select Mystery Gift and accept the Wonder Card(s) | You can claim multiple cards at once |
| 4 | Enter your save file and save the game | This is the step people skip — do not skip it |
| 5 | Travel to any PokéMart | Every PokéMart uses the same delivery NPC |
| 6 | Talk to the NPC on the left in green mailman attire | He is the deliveryman for all gifts |
| 7 | Collect your Pokémon or items | Randomized stats are rolled right here |

### Why the Save Step Matters So Much

The developers built in a specific rule for this feature: if you want to claim a gift on multiple save files, or you plan to soft-reset for a better Pokémon, you must enter the game and save after claiming from the Main Menu. If you switch save files or close the program without doing that, the game does not register the gift as claimed, and you will have to claim it again. It is not a bug — it is how the distribution flag is written.

### If Your Gift Pokémon Looks Wrong

Community reports mention one recurring cosmetic issue: a gift Pokémon that arrives without its special sprite. The standard fix is to run the patcher. On the Main Menu, use the Check For Updates option so the client downloads the latest data files, then pick up the gift again. Keeping the game patched also ensures you are seeing the most recent distribution rather than an expired card.

## Soft-Resetting Your Gift: What Is Random and What Is Fixed

This is the part that makes the Pokemon Uranium Mystery Gift system genuinely useful for competitive-minded players. The developers split the data into two stages: the card itself is fixed the moment you claim it, but the Pokémon's randomized traits are not decided until you actually receive it from the deliveryman. That gap is your window to reroll.

| Decided When You Claim on the Main Menu | Decided When You Pick Up at the PokéMart |
| --- | --- |
| Which gift you receive | Nature |
| The Wonder Card and its recorded date | Ability |
| The distribution's fixed held item and moves | Shininess |
| The fixed level, ball, and ID number | Gender (player experience) |
| — | Individual values (player experience, not officially documented) |

A practical soft-reset routine looks like this:

- Claim the gift from the Main Menu, then enter your save and save the game.
- Stand directly in front of the deliveryman and save a second time so your checkpoint is right there.
- Talk to him and check the summary screen. If the nature, ability, or color is wrong, reset the game and talk to him again.
- Repeat until you get what you want — you never have to re-download or re-claim the card.

Because the roll happens at pickup, this method is fast. Most players report that a single reset cycle takes well under a minute once they are standing in front of the NPC.

## Tips, Community Reports, and Troubleshooting

A few habits separate players who collect every gift from players who lose them:

- Claim on every save file you care about. Each file has its own claim flag, but each one needs its own post-claim save.
- Claim early in the window. Distributions rotate, and an unclaimed card can become unavailable.
- Check for updates before a soft-reset session so sprite data and gift flags are current.
- Keep your system clock in mind — the received date on the Wonder Card mirrors your computer's date.
- Back up your save file before long reset sessions, just in case.

| Problem | Likely Cause and Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Mystery Gift is missing from the Main Menu | You have not received the Pokédex yet |
| The deliveryman has nothing for you | You claimed the card but never saved; claim again, then save |
| The gift vanished after switching saves | The claim was not registered; claim it again and enter the game |
| The Pokémon has no special sprite | Run the patcher via Check For Updates on the Main Menu |
| The Wonder Card date looks wrong | The date follows your system clock, not the server |

For the most current download and update information, check the [official Pokemon Uranium website](https://pokemon-uranium.com/) before troubleshooting manually — an outdated client is the cause of most of the odd behavior players report.

## FAQ

**How do I unlock the Pokemon Uranium Mystery Gift option?**
You need to reach the point in the story where Professor Bamb'o gives you your Pokédex. Until that happens, the Mystery Gift entry stays hidden on the Main Menu, even if a distribution is live.

**Can a Mystery Gift Pokémon be shiny?**
Yes. Shininess, along with nature and ability, is rolled at the moment you receive the Pokémon from the PokéMart deliveryman. Save before talking to him, then reset and retry until you get the result you want.

**Can I claim the same gift on more than one save file?**
You can, but you must enter the game and save on each file after claiming from the Main Menu. Skipping that step means the gift is not registered, and you will have to claim it again.

**Do I need an internet connection or an account to use the Pokemon Uranium Mystery Gift feature?**
No account and no code are required. Gifts are delivered through the game client, so running Check For Updates is what keeps you aligned with whatever distribution is currently live. Player experience suggests checking for updates is the single most reliable troubleshooting step when a gift looks broken.
