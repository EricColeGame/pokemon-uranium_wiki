## How Evolution Works in Pokemon Uranium

The core system will feel familiar. A Pokemon that evolves by level gains the change automatically after the battle in which it reaches the required level. As in the official games, you can typically cancel the animation by pressing B, which is useful if you want to learn a move that the evolved form would otherwise skip.

A few details are worth remembering:

- Evolution checks happen after battle, so a Pokemon that levels up mid-fight will not transform until the fight ends.
- Rare Candies still trigger level-based evolutions, which makes them a fast way to test whether a species evolves by level at all.
- The in-game Pokedex records data for species you have seen or caught, and it is the most reliable place to confirm a specific requirement.
- Some species simply do not evolve, and a few have branching families where your choice of item or condition decides the final form.

Because Uranium is a community-maintained fan project, the definitive reference is the community wiki rather than any printed manual. The [Pokemon Uranium Wiki Pokedex](https://pokemon-uranium.fandom.com/wiki/Pok%C3%A9dex) lists every registered species along with its type, and it is the fastest starting point when you need to confirm a family line. From there you can check the official [Pokemon Uranium website](https://pokemonuranium.com/) for news and patch information that occasionally adjusts evolution behavior.

One important caveat: Uranium was built by fans over many years, and balance patches have changed details more than once. Anything you read about Pokemon Uranium evolution levels should be treated as community-reported unless you verify it in your own current build.

Orchynx is the defensive pick, gaining the Grass/Steel combination that gives it a long list of resistances once it becomes Metalynx. Raptorch leans offensive and fast, while Eletux offers a Water/Electric profile that is handy against several Tandor gym leaders.

Because all three sit near the same threshold, the decision is less about when they evolve and more about what you do in the levels before that. Two suggestions from player experience:

- Do not delay the level-up just to learn one more pre-evolution move unless that move is genuinely important to your build.
- Save your best TMs for the evolved form, since its stats and movepool will usually be better suited to them.

If your starter feels weak around the mid-20s, that is normal. The power spike arrives with the evolution, and Tandor's mid-game trainers are balanced with that in mind.

## Beyond Leveling Up: Stones, Friendship, Trades, and Nuclear Forms

Level-based evolution is only one route in Pokemon Uranium. Several other methods exist, and confusing them is the most common reason players end up over-leveled with an unevolved Pokemon.

| Method | How it works | Practical notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evolution stones | Use the correct stone from your bag on the Pokemon | Stones are limited, so check the requirement before spending one |
| Friendship | Level up while the Pokemon's happiness is high | Berries, walking, and avoiding faints all help |
| Trade evolution | The Pokemon changes when traded | Online services have changed over time, so verify current workarounds |
| Special conditions | Held items, story events, or unique encounters | Community reports vary; confirm in the Pokedex |
| Nuclear forms | Tied to Uranium's signature Nuclear type | Often obtained through story or special encounters rather than leveling |

Trade evolutions deserve a specific warning. Uranium's official online infrastructure is no longer what it once was, and the community has relied on patches and in-game alternatives to keep trade-based families obtainable. Before you build a team around a trade evolution, confirm the current method in your version of the game. Assuming a trade is possible when it is not is one of the fastest ways to waste an afternoon.

Nuclear-type species add another layer. Many of them are encountered or created through the story rather than through ordinary leveling, so do not assume that hitting a certain level will trigger a Nuclear transformation. When in doubt, check the wiki entry for the specific species.

## Practical Tips for Planning Around Pokemon Uranium Evolution Levels

The players who breeze through Tandor are not necessarily the ones who grind the hardest. They are the ones who plan their evolutions in advance. Use the checklist below before each major milestone.

| Situation | What to check first | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entering a new gym | Are your key Pokemon within a few levels of evolving? | The stat jump often decides the fight |
| About to use a Rare Candy | Does the species evolve by level? | Avoids wasting a rare item |
| About to use an evolution stone | Is this the only stone of its kind you have? | Some stones are scarce |
| Adding a late-game catch | What is its reported evolution level? | Determines whether it is worth the investment |
| Building around a trade evolution | Is the trade method currently available? | Prevents a dead-end team slot |

Beyond the checklist, a few habits pay off consistently:

- **Train a balanced core of four.** Uranium's level curve rewards a focused team over a scattered one, and fewer Pokemon means faster evolution progress.
- **Keep a backup for long middle stages.** Three-stage families can leave you with a mediocre middle form for many levels, so a second trained Pokemon smooths the gap.
- **Use the Pokedex before you commit.** The community-maintained Pokédex remains the best single reference for Pokemon Uranium evolution levels, and it is updated far more often than any guide written at launch.
- **Recheck after patches.** Fan projects evolve too, and evolution requirements occasionally shift between versions.

Finally, remember that an unevolved Pokemon is not a broken one. Some pre-evolution forms learn useful moves earlier, and a well-trained team can clear Tandor without rushing every evolution. The goal is to know the requirement, not to obsess over it.

## FAQ

**Do all Pokemon in Uranium evolve by level?**

No. Uranium uses a mix of level-up, stone, friendship, trade, and special-condition evolutions, plus its signature Nuclear forms. Leveling is the most common route, but it is far from the only one, which is why checking each species individually matters.

**What level do the Tandor starters evolve?**

Community reports consistently place the starter evolutions around level 30, later than what many players expect from the mainline games. Because balance patches have touched the game over the years, confirm the exact number in your current build.

**Can I use Rare Candies to trigger evolution?**

Yes, in most cases. A Rare Candy that pushes a Pokemon past its required level will trigger the evolution after the next battle, just like normal experience. It is also a quick way to test whether a species evolves by level at all.

**Where can I find exact Pokemon Uranium evolution levels for a specific species?**

The community wiki is the most reliable source, since it is maintained by active players and updated as patches release. Cross-check anything you find with your own in-game Pokedex, especially if you are running an older or modified version of the game.

Get the requirements right, plan your team around them, and Tandor becomes a much friendlier place. Knowing Pokemon Uranium evolution levels will not win battles for you, but it will make sure your strongest Pokemon are actually strong when it counts.
