## Why Trade Evolutions Matter in Pokemon Uranium

Pokemon Uranium keeps one of the oldest rules in the franchise: a small group of species will only evolve after they have been traded to another trainer. For solo players, Pokemon Uranium trade evolutions can feel like a locked door — the game's online population is far smaller than a mainline title's, so finding a stranger at the exact moment you need one is unreliable at best.

Getting Pokemon Uranium trade evolutions done is still worth the setup, though, because the payoff goes well beyond a couple of extra Pokedex entries. Trade-only species are often competitively useful, and the same file-swapping trick that triggers their evolution also unlocks multiple starters, bonus experience, extra lottery prizes, and second chances at rare items.

This guide walks through how trading works in the fan-made RPG, then breaks down the self-trading method that players have been documenting and refining on the community forums for years.

## How Trading and the GTS Work in Pokemon Uranium

Pokemon Uranium uses a Global Trade Station (GTS) hosted inside every Pokecenter. You deposit a Pokemon, attach a request, and another player — or another one of your own save files — can complete the trade later. There is no need for both players to be online at the same time, which is exactly what makes self-trading possible.

Two details matter more than anything else:

- **Deposits save automatically.** When you leave the GTS menu and return to the Pokecenter, the game writes your deposit to your save file. You do not have to save manually.
- **Every save file is a separate trainer.** Each file has its own trainer name, ID, and Pokemon storage, so the game treats your second playthrough as a legitimate trading partner.

That second point is the whole trick. According to community reports, a player with two save files can move Pokemon between them indefinitely, one trade at a time, without ever contacting another person.

One caveat worth stating up front: moderators on the community forum have flagged *duplicating and sharing* trade or save files as against the rules. Building a second file of your own and trading through the GTS is a different activity, but never pass save files around or trade away copies of someone else's data.

## The Self-Trading Method, Step by Step

The workflow below is adapted from a long-running community guide originally posted by a player named TheTainted_Wisdom. Treat it as player experience rather than official documentation — results can vary between versions.

| Step | What you do | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Back up your save folder before you begin | Cheap insurance; the delete option in the menu asks twice, but a backup removes all risk |
| 2 | Start a **New Game** on a spare file and play to the first town | The Pokecenter and GTS unlock very early |
| 3 | Save, return to the main menu, and claim any active Mystery Gifts | Gifts are tied to the file that claims them |
| 4 | Catch a throwaway species — Chyinmunk, Birbie, or Cubbug work well | Common, low-level, and nobody else is hunting them |
| 5 | Deposit that throwaway on the GTS and leave the menu | The deposit saves automatically when you return to the Pokecenter |
| 6 | From the main menu, open **Other Save Files** and load your main file | No need to close the game |
| 7 | Search the GTS for your throwaway, complete the trade, and deposit the next Pokemon | This is where the actual evolution happens |
| 8 | Swap files back and forth until everything sits where you want it | Slow but completely reliable |

The whole loop takes a couple of minutes per Pokemon once you get the rhythm down. Because you always deposit junk, you never risk losing something valuable to a stranger who happens to be browsing the same listing.

## Smart GTS Searching (and How to Avoid a Crash)

The Uranium GTS is small enough that a handful of species dominate the listings. Community reports note that searching for heavily requested Pokemon such as Archilles, Actan, and Lanthan can crash the game outright, simply because there are too many results to load. The fix is to search from the *rarer* side of the trade.

| You are offering | Search for instead | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A starter | Cubbug, Birbie, or Chyinmunk listings | The common side loads instantly and your listing is easy to spot |
| Actan | Baashaun listings | Avoids the overloaded Actan search |
| Anything high-demand | The species you actually want | Fewer results, less chance of a crash |

| GTS traffic | Species examples | Practical takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frequently offered | Chyinmunk, Birbie, Cubbug | Perfect throwaways; safe to deposit and forget |
| Frequently requested | Archilles, Actan, Lanthan | Never search these directly if you can help it |

One more habit worth building: **always check the trainer name before confirming a trade.** It is easy to grab the wrong listing and hand over a level 100 sweeper in exchange for a level 2 Birbie. If you deposit only garbage and confirm only your own trainer name, that mistake cannot happen.

## What Else Self-Trading Unlocks Beyond Evolutions

Trade evolutions are the headline feature, but they are far from the only benefit. The same file-swapping loop turns a lot of Uranium's one-per-save restrictions into repeatable rewards.

| Benefit | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Competitively viable trade evolutions | You control the IVs, nature, and training of both sides of the trade |
| All three starters on one file | Create a new file, pick a different starter, trade it over |
| Bonus experience | Traded Pokemon level faster, which speeds up repeat playthroughs considerably |
| Multiple Lottery prizes | The Lottery number is identical across all save files, so several characters in Bealbeach City can trade winning Pokemon to each other for PP Ups, Big Roots, and Master Balls |
| Extra legendary attempts | Community reports suggest you can create additional files for another shot at good IVs on legendaries like Urayne |
| Insurance for one-time items | Wasting a Royal Jelly, Razor Fang, Eviolite, or Binding Band stings less when you can farm a replacement on a spare file |
| Shiny and Nuclear dex credit | Move variants between files to fill out Pokedex entries you would otherwise miss |

The Mystery Gift side of the method is worth a separate note. If you claim a gift and retrieve it from the Pokemart deliveryman immediately, the deliveryman stays available on that file — which some players believe allows repeated attempts at a shiny gift, even after the event window closes. That claim is unverified community folklore, so treat it as a curiosity rather than a strategy. What is confirmed is simpler: if you claim a gift and then switch files without picking it up, the gift is gone.

## Risks, Rules, and Etiquette

Self-trading is powerful, but it is not risk-free. A short checklist keeps you out of trouble.

| Risk | How to handle it |
| --- | --- |
| Accidentally trading away a keeper | Deposit only throwaways; confirm the trainer name before accepting |
| Mystery Gift disappearing | Retrieve it from the deliveryman before leaving that save |
| GTS crash on popular searches | Search the rarer species in the pair, never the overloaded one |
| Rule violations | Do not duplicate or share save files; that is what moderators have warned against |
| Lost progress | Copy your save folder to a safe location before experimenting |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I complete Pokemon Uranium trade evolutions without a second player?**
Yes. The standard community method is to create a second save file, deposit a common Pokemon on the GTS, then switch to your main file and trade for it. Repeat the swap until every Pokemon you want to evolve has been traded.

**Do traded Pokemon really earn more experience in Uranium?**
According to player experience, yes. Traded Pokemon receive a boosted experience rate, which is one of the main reasons players move their teams between files early rather than late.

**Why does the GTS crash when I search for certain Pokemon?**
Community reports point to listing volume. Species that are requested constantly — Archilles, Actan, and Lanthan are the usual examples — can return too many results for the game to handle, so search from the common side of the trade instead.

**Is self-trading against the rules?**
Building your own extra save file and trading through the GTS is the documented community approach. What moderators have explicitly warned against is duplicating and sharing trade or save files with other people, so keep your files to yourself and you stay on the right side of the guidelines.

**Do I need to save manually before switching files?**
No. Leaving the GTS menu and returning to the Pokecenter saves your deposit automatically. Just make sure any Mystery Gift you claim is picked up before you switch, or it will not be there when you come back.
