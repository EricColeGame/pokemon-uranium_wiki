## Tips for Completing Pokemon Uranium All Pokemon

Filling the entire dex is a long project, but it is very doable if you work in the right order. These habits come from player experience and community discussion rather than any official checklist.

- Catch everything on a route before you leave it. Backtracking across Tandor costs far more time than throwing a few extra balls.
- Track evolution families, not individual species. If you own one member of a line, you can usually breed or train your way to the rest.
- Keep a dedicated "dex box" in storage so you never accidentally release something you still need.
- Note one-time encounters immediately. If an area is story-locked, that species may not come back.
- Use the community stat lists when you are choosing what to raise next, especially for base stats and EV yield.

## FAQ

**How many Pokemon are in the Pokemon Uranium all Pokemon roster?**
Community counts vary, but the numbered Tandor dex runs well past 150 entries and includes both original fan-designed species and official Pokemon. Add evolutions and alternate forms and the realistic completion target is higher still.

**Which starter is best for a first playthrough?**
Orchynx is the most forgiving pick thanks to its Grass/Steel typing and defensive stats. Raptorch hits harder but dies faster, and Eletux sits comfortably in the middle as a balanced special attacker.

**Do I need to catch Nuclear-type Pokemon to finish the dex?**
Yes, if you are going for full completion. They occupy their own slots in the dex and are tied to specific story areas, so plan those catches around the main plot rather than saving them for the end.

**Can I complete the Pokemon Uranium all Pokemon dex without trading?**
The dex is designed around a single-player experience, so the vast majority of entries are obtainable on your own save. The main obstacles are one-time encounters and evolution conditions, not trades.
