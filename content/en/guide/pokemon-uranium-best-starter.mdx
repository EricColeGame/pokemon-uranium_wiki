Choosing a first partner in the Tandor region is not the throwaway decision it is in some fan games. The Pokemon Uranium best starter question comes up in every forum thread and Discord server because Eletux, Raptorch, and Orchynx run into very different walls on the road to the Championship. Pick well and the eight-gym stretch feels manageable; pick badly and a couple of fights turn into genuine roadblocks.

Below is a practical breakdown of the Pokemon Uranium best starter options, built from community analysis and player experience. It covers gym matchups, rival battles, Nuclear-type threats, and how the trio performs once you take the fight online.

## Meet the Three Starters of the Tandor Region

Every run begins with a choice in Moki Town, and each of the three starters grows into a completely different role by the time you reach the late game.

**Eletux** is the balanced pick. Its Water/Electric typing gives it a mixed attacking profile that eventually becomes Electruxo. It handles the earliest rival battles comfortably and has one of the cleanest matchups in the game against Tiko's Fire-type gym. The catch is that it rarely dominates — community analyses describe it as running roughly even with most major opponents rather than blowing them open.

**Raptorch** is the offensive pick. Fire/Ground coverage hits hard, and its final form, Archilles, is one of the fastest Pokémon in Tandor. The trade-off is fragility and inconsistency: Raptorch can flatten one gym almost single-handedly, then struggle badly against a Water-type leader.

**Orchynx** is the tank. Grass/Steel is a defensive typing loaded with resistances, and its evolution Metalynx can Mega Evolve according to community reports. The downside is speed and one brutal Fire-type matchup where it should simply stay in its ball.

| Starter | Type | Final Evolution | Battle Role | Ideal For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eletux | Water / Electric | Electruxo | Balanced mixed attacker | Players who want a safe, flexible run |
| Raptorch | Fire / Ground | Archilles | Fast physical sweeper | Players who want speed and pressure |
| Orchynx | Grass / Steel | Metalynx | Bulky defensive pivot | Players who want to absorb hits and grind wins |

## Story Play vs. Competitive Play: Two Answers to the Pokemon Uranium Best Starter Question

Here is where the debate gets interesting. The starter that makes the story easiest is not the starter that wins online battles, and the community is fairly unified about both rankings.

| Category | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story playthrough (community analysis) | Orchynx / Metalynx | Raptorch / Archilles | Eletux / Electruxo |
| Competitive or online play (community reports) | Raptorch / Archilles | Eletux / Electruxo | Orchynx / Metalynx |

**Why Archilles wins competitively:** community players point to its Speed tier, its access to Swords Dance, and a Mega Evolution that sets sun — turning its Fire attacks into genuine nukes. It also outspeeds most of the Nuclear-type threats you meet during the story, which makes it the safest bet against fast, fragile opponents.

**Why Electruxo ranks second:** it has the classic Bolt Beam combination, a Mega ability that effectively guarantees accurate Electric STAB, and Magnet Rise to patch up its Ground weakness. Players describe it as an excellent all-around special attacker that anchors rain-oriented teams.

**Why Metalynx slips to third online:** it is a wall rather than a sweeper. With defensive investment it can survive hits that would flatten the other two, and it wears opponents down with Leech Seed and Toxic. It is also the only starter that takes neutral damage from Nuclear-type moves, which makes it uniquely durable in a region full of them.

## Matching the Pokemon Uranium Best Starter to Your Playstyle

Statistically, the three starters are closer than the arguments suggest. Community analysts have repeatedly noted that the gap between first and third place is narrow — measured in decimals, not whole tiers. That means the right pick usually depends on how you like to battle.

| Your Priority | Best Pick | Why It Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Fewest surprises on a first run | Orchynx | Most resistances, best overall gym record |
| Fast, aggressive battles | Raptorch | Highest Speed and strong STAB coverage |
| Flexibility and coverage options | Eletux | Balanced stats with an Electric secondary type |
| Online battles and team synergy | Raptorch (or Eletux) | Mega Archilles and rain-team Electruxo |

A few practical tips regardless of your choice:

- **Invest EVs deliberately.** Metalynx rewards defensive investment, while Archilles wants Speed and Attack. A generic spread wastes their biggest strengths.
- **Plan for your bad matchup early.** Raptorch players should catch a Water or Grass answer before Cali; Orchynx players should have a Fire or Ground answer before Tiko.
- **Do not skip the Mega Evolution.** Community reports highlight that all three final forms have Mega options, and they change how the Pokémon functions in the late game.
- **Remember Nuclear typing.** It hits almost everything hard, so a bulky pivot on your team is worth more than a second glass cannon.

Pairing it with a defensive starter like Metalynx creates a genuinely balanced core.

If you picked Raptorch, prioritize something that can take a Water attack. If you picked Eletux, look for a hard-hitting physical attacker to complement its special-leaning output. Orchynx players have the most freedom here because Metalynx already handles the defensive job.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Pokemon Uranium Best Starter

**Is Orchynx really the Pokemon Uranium best starter?**
For the main story, community analysis puts it first by a narrow margin, mainly because of its resistance-heavy typing and strong gym record. For competitive play, most experienced players rank Archilles above it.

**Which starter is easiest for the first few gyms?**
Orchynx. It resists or takes neutral damage from most early gym attacks and can hit several opponents super effectively, which makes the opening stretch of Tandor noticeably smoother.

**Do I need to pick the "best" starter to finish the game?**
No. Community consensus is that the gap between the three is small and that any starter is viable in-story with a decent moveset and sensible EV investment. The choice changes the difficulty curve, not whether the game is beatable.

**Which starter handles Nuclear-type threats best?**
Orchynx fares best defensively because it takes neutral damage from Nuclear moves and has the bulk to survive a hit. Raptorch is the best offensively since it usually outspeeds and knocks Nuclear-types out before they can strike back.

Whichever starter you choose, the real difference-maker is how you build the team around it. Pick a partner that patches your weaknesses, plan for your one bad gym, and Tandor becomes a much friendlier place.
